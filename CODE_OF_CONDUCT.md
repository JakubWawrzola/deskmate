# Code of conduct

Deskmate is a small project run by one person, with help from everyone who
reports bugs, asks for features and sends fixes. Keep it a place where people
like to come back.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, also with people who are new to Home Assistant or to
  GitHub.
- Assume good intent. A short or blunt message is often just a busy person.
- Stay on topic: issues are for Deskmate, discussions for questions and ideas.
- Give credit, and accept that a request can be declined or postponed.

## Not accepted

- Insults, harassment, personal attacks, discrimination of any kind.
- Sexual language or imagery, threats, doxxing (posting someone's private
  details).
- Spam, advertising, repeated off-topic posts.
- Pushing others to share keys, tokens, pairing codes or logs with secrets.

## What happens

The maintainer may edit or delete comments, lock threads, and block accounts
that break these rules, with a warning first where that makes sense.

## Reporting

Use *Report content* on the comment (the three dots menu on GitHub), or
contact the maintainer, [@JakubWawrzola](https://github.com/JakubWawrzola),
through GitHub. Reports are handled privately.

This code applies to the issues, discussions, pull requests and every other
place where the project is represented.
